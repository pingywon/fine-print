1. MEMBERSHIP TERM. This membership has a minimum term of twelve (12) months beginning on the Membership Start Date (the "Commitment Period"). After the Commitment Period, this membership will continue on a month-to-month basis until cancelled in accordance with Section 6.

2. AUTOMATIC PAYMENTS. You authorize IronWorks Fitness Club ("IronWorks") to charge the credit card, debit card, or bank account on file for all dues, fees, and charges under this Agreement on the dates they are due, without further notice. If a payment is declined, a declined payment fee of $20.00 will be charged, and IronWorks may re-attempt the payment, including the fee, at any time. If you update your payment method with your bank or card issuer, IronWorks may obtain the updated account information and continue charging it.

3. ANNUAL CLUB ENHANCEMENT FEE. The Annual Club Enhancement Fee is charged each year on February 15 to all active members, including members in their first year, and is not prorated or refundable. The first Annual Club Enhancement Fee under this Agreement will be charged on February 15, 2027.

4. PRICE CHANGES. IronWorks may increase monthly dues, the Annual Club Enhancement Fee, or other fees after the Commitment Period by posting notice in the club at least thirty (30) days before the increase takes effect. Continued use of the club after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the new rate.

5. FREEZING YOUR MEMBERSHIP. You may freeze your membership for one (1) to three (3) months per year by submitting a request in person at your Home Club. A freeze fee of $10.00 per month applies. Your Commitment Period will be extended by the length of any freeze.

6. CANCELLATION. You may cancel your membership after the Commitment Period by (a) delivering a completed cancellation form in person at your Home Club, or (b) sending a written cancellation request by certified mail, return receipt requested, to IronWorks Member Services, P.O. Box 2210, Riverton, PA 17099. Cancellation requests made by telephone, email, the IronWorks app, or through any third party will not be accepted. Cancellation takes effect thirty (30) days after IronWorks receives your request, and you will be charged any dues and fees that come due during those thirty (30) days.

7. EARLY CANCELLATION. If you cancel during the Commitment Period, you must pay an early cancellation fee of $99.00 plus all remaining monthly dues for the Commitment Period, unless you qualify for a cancellation under Section 8.

8. CANCELLATION RIGHTS UNDER LAW. You may cancel this Agreement without penalty within three (3) business days of signing by delivering written notice to your Home Club. You may also cancel if you relocate more than twenty-five (25) miles from any IronWorks club, with proof of your new address, or if a physician certifies that you cannot use the club for medical reasons, with a written statement from the physician.

9. CLUB CLOSURES. IronWorks may close all or part of any club for maintenance, renovation, holidays, weather, or events beyond its control. Membership dues will continue to be charged during any closure of forty-five (45) days or less, and no credit or refund will be given.

10. PERSONAL TRAINING. Personal training sessions are sold separately. All personal training sessions must be used within ninety (90) days of purchase and expire after that date with no refund. Sessions cancelled with less than twenty-four (24) hours' notice will be forfeited.

11. GUEST PRIVILEGES. Premier members may bring one (1) guest per visit. Guests must be at least eighteen (18) years old, present valid photo identification, and sign a guest waiver. IronWorks may limit or end guest privileges at any time.

12. CLUB RULES. You agree to follow all posted club rules, which IronWorks may change at any time. IronWorks may suspend or terminate your membership for violation of club rules, and no refund will be given for unused dues or fees.

13. ASSUMPTION OF RISK AND RELEASE. You understand that physical exercise involves the risk of injury. You voluntarily assume all risks of using the club, its equipment, and its services, and you release IronWorks, its owners, employees, and agents from any and all claims for injury, illness, death, or property loss arising from your membership, including claims arising from the negligence of IronWorks or its employees.

14. LOCKERS AND PERSONAL PROPERTY. Day-use lockers must be emptied each day. IronWorks is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged property. Items left in day-use lockers overnight may be removed and discarded.

15. ARBITRATION. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement or your membership will be resolved by binding individual arbitration administered by a provider selected by IronWorks. You waive any right to a jury trial and any right to participate in a class action.

16. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Agreement, together with the posted club rules, is the entire agreement between you and IronWorks. No oral promises by any IronWorks employee are binding.
