RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Residential Lease Agreement ("Lease") is entered into on October 6, 2026, by and between Brightwater Property Group, LLC ("Landlord") and Jordan Ellis ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant the residential dwelling located at 214 Linden Street, Unit 3B, Riverton, PA 17099 (the "Premises"), together with one (1) assigned parking space, No. 14.

2. TERM. The term of this Lease begins on November 1, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. on October 31, 2027 (the "Initial Term").

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL. Unless Tenant delivers written notice of non-renewal to Landlord not less than ninety (90) days prior to the expiration of the Initial Term or any renewal term, this Lease shall automatically renew for an additional term of twelve (12) months on the same terms, except that Monthly Rent for any renewal term may be increased by up to eight percent (8%) at Landlord's sole discretion. Notice of non-renewal must be sent by certified mail to Landlord's address listed in Section 24. Notice by email or text message shall not be effective.

4. RENT. Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,850.00 ("Monthly Rent"), due in advance on the first (1st) day of each month, without demand, deduction, or offset. Rent shall be paid through Landlord's online resident portal. A convenience fee of $4.95 applies to each payment made by debit or credit card. Rent for any partial month shall be prorated on a daily basis.

5. LATE CHARGES. If any installment of rent is not received by Landlord by 11:59 p.m. on the third (3rd) day of the month, Tenant shall pay an initial late charge of $75.00, plus an additional late charge of $15.00 per day for each day thereafter until rent is paid in full. Any payment returned for insufficient funds shall incur a fee of $50.00, and Landlord may thereafter require that all payments be made by certified funds.

6. SECURITY DEPOSIT. Upon signing this Lease, Tenant shall pay a security deposit of $3,700.00. Landlord may apply the security deposit to unpaid rent, damage beyond normal wear and tear, cleaning, and any other amounts owed under this Lease. Landlord shall return the balance of the security deposit, with an itemized list of deductions, within sixty (60) days after Tenant vacates the Premises and returns all keys. A non-refundable cleaning fee of $350.00 shall be deducted from the security deposit at move-out regardless of the condition of the Premises.

7. UTILITIES. Landlord shall provide water, sewer, and trash removal. Tenant shall establish service in Tenant's own name and pay for electricity, natural gas, internet, and cable television. A monthly utility administration fee of $12.00 shall be added to Monthly Rent.

8. USE AND OCCUPANCY. The Premises shall be used only as a private residence for Tenant and the following additional occupants: none. Guests may not stay at the Premises for more than fourteen (14) days in any six-month period without Landlord's prior written consent.

9. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS. Tenant shall keep the Premises clean and in good condition. Tenant shall be responsible for the cost of all repairs to the Premises, including the repair or replacement of appliances, plumbing fixtures, and HVAC filters, where the cost of any single repair does not exceed $250.00, regardless of the cause of the damage. Landlord shall be responsible for repairs exceeding $250.00, except where the damage was caused by Tenant, Tenant's guests, or Tenant's negligence, in which case Tenant shall pay the full cost. Tenant shall report any water leak, mold, or pest activity to Landlord within twenty-four (24) hours of discovery.

10. ALTERATIONS. Tenant shall not paint, install fixtures, change locks, or make any alterations to the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. Any approved alterations shall become the property of Landlord.

11. LANDLORD'S ACCESS. Landlord and Landlord's agents may enter the Premises at any reasonable time to inspect, make repairs, show the Premises to prospective tenants or buyers, or for any other purpose Landlord deems necessary. Landlord will attempt to provide notice when practical, but notice is not required.

12. PETS. No animals of any kind shall be kept on the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent. If consent is given, Tenant shall pay a non-refundable pet fee of $400.00 and monthly pet rent of $45.00 per animal. An unauthorized animal shall result in a charge of $250.00 per occurrence.

13. SMOKING. Smoking of any substance, including vaping, is prohibited anywhere on the Premises and within twenty-five (25) feet of the building.

14. RENTERS INSURANCE. Tenant shall maintain renters insurance with personal liability coverage of not less than $100,000.00 for the entire term and shall name Landlord as an additional interested party. Tenant shall provide proof of coverage before receiving keys. If Tenant fails to maintain coverage, Landlord may purchase coverage on Tenant's behalf and charge Tenant the cost plus an administrative fee of $25.00 per month.

15. EARLY TERMINATION. If Tenant vacates the Premises before the end of the term for any reason, Tenant shall remain liable for all Monthly Rent due for the remainder of the term, and Landlord shall have no obligation to re-rent the Premises. In addition, Tenant shall pay a re-letting fee equal to one (1) month's rent.

16. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING. Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any part of the Premises, including through short-term rental platforms, without Landlord's prior written consent, which Landlord may withhold for any reason.

17. MOVE-OUT. Tenant shall return the Premises in the same condition as at the start of the term, ordinary wear and tear excepted, and shall have the carpets professionally cleaned by a vendor approved by Landlord, with a receipt provided to Landlord at move-out.

18. RULES AND REGULATIONS. Tenant shall comply with the Community Rules attached as Exhibit A, which Landlord may amend from time to time upon posting notice in the building lobby or on the resident portal. Quiet hours are from 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m.

19. DEFAULT. If Tenant fails to pay rent when due or breaches any other term of this Lease, Landlord may terminate this Lease and pursue all remedies available at law.

20. ATTORNEY'S FEES. In any action or proceeding arising out of this Lease, Tenant shall pay all of Landlord's reasonable attorney's fees, court costs, and collection costs, whether or not suit is filed.

21. WAIVER OF JURY TRIAL. Landlord and Tenant each waive the right to a trial by jury in any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Lease.

22. LIMITATION OF LANDLORD'S LIABILITY. Landlord shall not be liable for any loss of or damage to Tenant's personal property, or for any injury to Tenant or Tenant's guests, arising from any cause whatsoever, including water damage, theft, or the failure of building systems, except to the extent required by law.

23. HOLDOVER. If Tenant remains in possession after the end of the term without a renewal, Tenant shall pay rent at one hundred fifty percent (150%) of the most recent Monthly Rent for each month or portion of a month of holdover.

24. NOTICES. Notices to Landlord shall be sent to Brightwater Property Group, LLC, 900 Market Street, Suite 400, Riverton, PA 17099. Notices to Tenant may be delivered to the Premises or by email to the address on file.

25. ENTIRE AGREEMENT. This Lease, including Exhibit A, is the entire agreement between the parties and may only be modified in writing signed by both parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force.

LANDLORD: Brightwater Property Group, LLC
